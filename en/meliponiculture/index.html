Native stingless bees

Stingless Beekeeping

Observation, ecology and a direct way to stay close to biodiversity.

My interest in stingless beekeeping comes mainly from the role native stingless bees play in pollination and biodiversity. It offers a chance to observe ecological relationships that often remain invisible in everyday life.

More than honey production, what interests me is following the colonies, noticing flowering cycles and maintaining a closer connection with local ecology.

How I see the activity

Three essential points

Biodiversity

Native bees participate in pollination networks that support local vegetation and ecological resilience.

Observation

Colonies and flowering patterns reveal environmental rhythms, resource availability and seasonal change.

Responsible management

Colony welfare, region-appropriate species and interventions compatible with the bees' needs come before production.
