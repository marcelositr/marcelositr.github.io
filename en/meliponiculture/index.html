Stingless Beekeeping

Environmental observation, biodiversity and responsible management.

My interest in stingless beekeeping comes mainly from the role native stingless bees play in pollination and biodiversity. More than honey production, what interests me is following the colonies, noticing flowering cycles and maintaining a closer connection with local ecology.

01 / Principles

Three points that guide the activity.

01

Biodiversity

Native bees participate in pollination networks that support local vegetation and ecological resilience.

02

Observation

Colonies and flowering patterns reveal environmental rhythms, resource availability and seasonal change.

03

Responsible management

Colony welfare, region-appropriate species and interventions compatible with the bees' needs come before production.
