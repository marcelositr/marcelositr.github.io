01 / About

I prefer what I do to the title placed before my name.

My formal background is environmental, but I have never enjoyed fitting into a single field. I am curious, observant and strongly practical: when something catches my attention, I usually study it, test it, take the idea apart and return to it as many times as needed.

Technology has been part of my life since the early 2000s. Environmental studies came later as both training and a way of looking at territory, water, cities and the relationships between systems. In practice, the two paths often meet: measure, observe, understand and improve something that exists in the real world.

Outside the technical side, music has been with me since I was nine. I also dedicate time to family, Jewish study and practice, history, astronomy, cooking and daily walks with ATKA, my Siberian Husky. I enjoy people, conversation and knowledge that circulates without becoming a pedestal.

02 / How I think

The same process shows up in almost everything.

The subjects change. The way I approach them changes much less.

  1. 01Observe

    Understand the context before changing things.

  2. 02Test

    Experiment in a practical, small-scale way.

  3. 03Understand

    Find out why something worked or why it did not.

  4. 04Adjust

    Refine, measure again and return as often as needed.

  5. 05Share

    Let knowledge circulate when it can help someone.
